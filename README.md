**PandaFloristBot** is a cozy 3D Low-Poly tabletop diorama flower garden and bouquet-crafting game built as a high-performance **Telegram Mini App**. 

Step into the paws of a gentle Panda Florist tending a wooden tabletop diorama with raised garden beds, a bamboo-roofed flower stall, visiting animal customers, traveling caravan merchants, and interactive garden ornaments.

---

## 🎮 How to Play (For Players)

No downloads or installs required. Your progress automatically saves to your device.

Any Telegram user can jump right into the garden in **three ways**:

1. **Direct Mini App Link:**
   Tap [https://t.me/PandaFloristBot/garden](https://t.me/PandaFloristBot/garden) to launch immediately inside Telegram on iOS, Android, or Telegram Desktop.
2. **Search in Telegram:**
   Search for **`@PandaFloristBot`** in Telegram and tap **Open** or the bottom-left **Menu Button** (Play).
3. **Open a Friend's Gift Link:**
   When a friend shares a bouquet link (`https://t.me/PandaFloristBot/garden?startapp=g_...`), tapping it launches the app and immediately displays an animated **Gift Box Modal 🎁** with the custom hand-crafted bouquet, bonus rare seeds, and coins!
